About AI Film Frontiers

AI Film Frontiers (AIFF) explores how generative AI is transforming the filmmaking pipeline—expanding artistic expression while redefining how creators collaborate with AI throughout the creative process. The workshop highlights AI-native filmmaking workflows and investigates key technical challenges, including character consistency, multi-shot narrative coherence, audio-visual generation, controllability, and effective human–AI collaboration.

AIFF features an international AI Film Challenge. Creators submit an AI-generated film together with a Technical Report that documents the ideas, tools, human interventions, technical challenges, and workflow innovations behind the production. Award-winning films and their reports will be presented at SIGGRAPH Asia 2026: films will be screened during the workshop, and accepted Technical Reports will be published in the SIGGRAPH Asia 2026 Proceedings.

Submission

A complete entry consists of two coordinated submissions: the film and its accompanying Technical Report.

1. AI-Generated Film

Submit the finished film through FilmFreeway. The dedicated AIFF FilmFreeway page and detailed film requirements will be announced here.

Submission channel: FilmFreeway

2. Technical Report

Submit the report through the SIGGRAPH Submission System. Reports must use the SIGGRAPH Proceedings template with the sigconf style.

Submission channel: SIGGRAPH Asia Linklings

Technical Report Requirements

  • Project Overview: Introduce the project, its motivation, and its creative objectives.
  • Creative Workflow: Describe the complete production pipeline, including AI models, software, tools, and human intervention.
  • Technical Problems and Solutions: Explain the major challenges—such as character consistency, multi-shot storytelling, audio-visual synchronization, and controllability—and the solutions or workflow innovations used to address them.
  • Copyright Declaration: State the ownership and licensing status of all assets used in the project, following the declaration template in the submission instructions.

Review Process

Final winners will be selected through a combined artistic and technical review.

1

Eligibility Check

Organizers verify that both the film and Technical Report have been submitted and that the entry satisfies the announced requirements.

2

Artistic Review

Reviewers evaluate cinematic quality, storytelling, originality, creativity, and the strength of the film's artistic expression.

3

Technical Review

Reviewers assess workflow design, the clarity of the report, the significance of the technical challenges, and the novelty and effectiveness of the proposed solutions.

Award decisions will consider both review tracks. Selected films and their corresponding reports will be presented together at SIGGRAPH Asia 2026.
